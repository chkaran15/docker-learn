The process

Bar stock to blade

01

Forge

~2,000°F

High-carbon bar stock is drawn out under the hammer into a rough profile — spine, belly, and tip roughed in while the steel still moves.

02

Grind

Cold, freehand

The bevel and spine geometry are established on the belt grinder, checked by eye and by hand against light, not a jig.

03

Harden

1,475°F quench

One pass through the critical temperature, then a fast quench in oil or water depending on the steel. This is the only step with no second chance.

04

Temper

400–600°F, twice

The blade goes back into the oven to draw out brittleness. We read the oxide color on the steel itself as our thermometer — straw for hardness, blue for toughness.

05

Finish

Hand work

Hand-sanded, etched to reveal the grain, handle fitted, and honed to a working edge before it ever leaves the shop.
